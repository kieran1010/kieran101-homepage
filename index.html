Who I am

Clinician, researcher,
and musician

I am a doctor with experience across anaesthesia, critical care, general medicine, retrievals and transfer medicine, and a fellow of both the Royal College of Anaesthetists (UK) and the Australian and New Zealand College of Anaesthetists. Originally trained as a biochemist, I completed doctoral and postdoctoral research in the UK before returning to medical school in 2010. After relocating to New Zealand in 2021, I worked in a range of regional and national anaesthetic departments and ICUs before joining the department of anaesthesia and pain medicine at Wellington Regional Hospital, where I worked until July 2026. I am currently working as a locum consultant anaesthetist in various anaesthetic departments around New Zealand. I hold additional positions as a Senior Clinical Lecturer at the University of Otago, a flight doctor with New Zealand Air Ambulance Service, and an event medic with St John Ambulance.

Education & teaching

Teaching

Teaching resources, slide decks and materials produced for undergraduate medical students, postgraduate trainees and GP education. Includes clinical web tools, a Miro board for interactive teaching, and a library of presentations available to download from Google Drive.

Synapse Anaesthetic Simulator
A browser-based anaesthesia teaching simulator. Manage a patient through surgery using a realistic monitor display and drug deck — adjusting ventilation, giving boluses and infusions, and responding to triggered clinical events. Designed for self-directed study and trainee teaching.
Open simulator ↗
Vantage Theatre Toolkit
A clinical web app for anaesthesia. Combines drug dosing calculators, a local anaesthetic toxicity tracker, paediatric drug doses, opioid conversion, anticoagulant guidance, POCUS references, and emergency resources in a single mobile-friendly interface.
Open toolkit ↗
Interactive Miro Board
A shared Miro whiteboard with teaching diagrams, frameworks and interactive exercises for clinical education. Click to open in Miro.
